Data and privacy

Your accounts and transactions are stored in this browser's IndexedDB database (existing localStorage data is migrated automatically). Nothing is sent to a server. Data remains on this device and browser; clearing browser data can erase it. Use the JSON backup regularly, especially before changing devices or browsers.
